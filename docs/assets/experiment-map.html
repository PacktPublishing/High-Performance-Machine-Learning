HPML Book Experiment Map

How each chapter's experiments attach to the book's running example, the RAG system: BGE-M3 encodes the documents into a FAISS index, a query retrieves the top passages, and a generator writes the answer. The generator grows from Gemma-2B to Llama-3.1-8B and Qwen3-VL-8B, and in Chapter 12 the system becomes a multimodal agent over PDF page images. All twelve chapters are measured.

Threads across the book

  • The decode bottleneck is found in Chapter 2, explained in Chapters 3 and 5, and reduced three ways: per-step overhead (Chapter 4), batching under load (Chapter 8) and fewer bytes per token (Chapter 9).
  • The generator grows from Gemma-2B (Chapters 1–9) to Llama-3.1-8B (Chapters 8–10) to Qwen3-VL-8B (Chapters 10 and 12).
  • Training the generator is planned in Chapter 7 and carried out in Chapter 10 with LoRA, QLoRA and full fine-tuning, including FSDP on two GPUs.
  • The retriever and index are measured in Chapters 2–3 and extended to page images in Chapter 12; Chapter 10 shows that new facts belong in the index, not in the weights.
  • Hardware is T4, A100 and H100 through Chapter 9; Chapter 11 adds TPU v5e and v6e, and Chapter 12 compares the H100 with the L40S and A100 on cost per task.

Values are from the chapter manuscripts; Chapters 10–12 were measured in November 2026.
