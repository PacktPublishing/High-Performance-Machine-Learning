# About the author

<div class="author" markdown>

<div markdown>

**Dr. Kaoutar El Maghraoui** is a Principal Research Scientist at the IBM T.J. Watson Research Center, where she
is a principal investigator in the IBM Research AI Hardware Center. Her work covers AI hardware and software
co-design, efficient LLM inference, analog in-memory computing and neural architecture search.

She is an Adjunct Professor of Computer Science at Columbia University, where she teaches the graduate courses
*High-Performance Machine Learning* and *Scaling LLMs*. This book grew out of the High-Performance Machine
Learning course.

She has more than 70 publications and 17 patents. She is an ACM Distinguished Member and ACM Distinguished
Speaker, and an IEEE Senior Member.
